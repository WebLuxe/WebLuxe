Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. We only get your details if you choose to message us.

Who we are

WebLuxe is a web design studio based in Mauritius. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: buildyourdreamwebsitedotcom@gmail.com or WhatsApp +230 5753 4025.

Cookies and storage on your device

We do not set any cookies. If you pick a currency, your browser remembers it using local storage, so prices stay in that currency next time:

  • wl_cur — the currency you chose.
  • wl_fx — today’s exchange rates, kept for 12 hours so the page loads faster.

These stay on your device, are never sent to us, and only exist to provide the feature you used. You can clear them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

The contact form and quote tool

The contact form and quote tool do not send anything to us by themselves. They prepare a message that you then choose to send through WhatsApp or your email app. When you do, we receive your name, the contact details you use, and your message.

We use this only to reply to you, prepare a quote and, if you hire us, deliver your project. We never sell it or use it for marketing lists. We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to answer you and follow up. If you become a client, we keep project and billing records for as long as Mauritian accounting and tax law requires. Our legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

Messages are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) or Gmail (Google), under their own privacy policies.

Services this website relies on

Like any website, these services see your IP address and basic browser information when your browser contacts them:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site and may keep short-term server logs for security.
  • Unsplash serves some of the photos.
  • ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) provides the live currency rates.

Fonts and code libraries are hosted on this site itself, so they are not loaded from Google or other third parties. Some of these providers are outside Mauritius and the EU.

Your rights

Under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 and, where it applies, the EU/UK GDPR, you can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Just email or WhatsApp us. You can also complain to the Data Protection Office of Mauritius or your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, for example by adding cookie-free visitor statistics, we will update this page and its date first.
